How the stay could unfold

Morning might begin in Monte Carlo, with breakfast on a terrace before a driver takes you towards Nice. Another day belongs to Cannes, or to the quieter roads behind the coast. Saint-Tropez deserves time of its own: in summer, we consider traffic and distances before suggesting a day trip or a second base.

Choose a private driver when you would rather watch the landscape, or a rental car for the pleasure of stopping whenever you wish. We can coordinate hotels, breakfast and lunch reservations, exceptional dinners, sightseeing, nightlife and celebrations, with a thoughtful gift waiting on arrival.

A day on the water can be modest and intimate or more expansive. We present small skippered boats, sailing yachts and larger motor yachts according to your budget, group and preferred atmosphere. You choose; we confirm the vessel, crew, inclusions and conditions before arranging the day.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
