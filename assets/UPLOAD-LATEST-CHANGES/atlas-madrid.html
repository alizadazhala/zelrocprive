How the stay could unfold

Choose a central base and Madrid begins to unfold on foot: the Prado, the shade of the Retiro, a pause over coffee before another gallery. Leave space between visits. Lunch runs late here, and an evening can move naturally from an aperitif to dinner and music.

Mandarin Oriental Ritz, Madrid places you beside the Prado; Rosewood Villa Magna offers a different base on Paseo de la Castellana. For dining, Deessa at the Ritz is an option for a considered gastronomic evening, while Horcher brings a classical dining-room atmosphere near the Retiro. These are editorial starting points, chosen for different moods, with reservations explored for your dates.

Bring your own favourite tables, or ask us to find something new. We can coordinate airport transfers, museum timings, restaurants and the journey home after an evening out. A single dinner merits the same care as a whole city stay.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.

Tell us which setting or property appeals, approximate dates and how many people are travelling. You do not need a complete itinerary.
