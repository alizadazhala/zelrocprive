Journal / Time together

Breakfast, at everyone’s pace

A few notes on sharing a house without sharing a timetable.

One person is up for a swim. Someone else is still reading in bed. The children are hungry before anyone has thought about lunch. A shared villa works best when there is room for these different days.

Before we look at houses, tell us who is coming and what makes time together easy: bedrooms close to one another, a quiet corner, step-free access, or one table large enough for everybody.

Where the property supports it, we can explore a chef, groceries before arrival and housekeeping. Share favourite breakfasts, dietary needs and the meals you would like to leave entirely to someone else.
