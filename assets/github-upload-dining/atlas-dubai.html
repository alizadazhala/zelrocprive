How the stay could unfold

Spend the morning by the water and leave the afternoon for one city outing. A Palm stay and a dinner across Dubai work best with generous travel time, rather than several crossings in a day.

At Bvlgari, the marina becomes part of the setting; at Atlantis The Royal, dining and pool time can shape much of the stay. A Raffles villa gives a group another kind of privacy. We compare the accommodation, then arrange the rhythm around your choice.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
