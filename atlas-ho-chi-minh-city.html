Times Square, Nguyễn Huệ Boulevard & Đồng Khởi Street, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

The Reverie Saigon

An expressive high-rise base for city life, with a particularly decorative return.

  • Elevated rooms and suites overlook the city or the Saigon River according to category.
  • The sixth-floor outdoor mosaic pool provides a pause above the street.
  • Long Triều’s Cantonese dining is distinct from the street-level mood of The Long @ Times Square.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Guests who favour decorative interiors and a central high-rise base over a small, understated house.

A stay, imagined

Step out towards Nguyễn Huệ before the city becomes hot, with a guide if you want the morning’s history to have a little more context. Return for lunch and a swim above the street, then leave time to enjoy the room rather than immediately setting out again. An evening at Long Triều can be a destination of its own. Another night might be simpler and closer to the boulevard. Choose the outlook and interior style consciously: the hotel’s decorative confidence is part of the experience.
