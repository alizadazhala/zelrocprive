PARKROYAL COLLECTION Marina Bay, Singapore

An urban garden hotel for guests who enjoy Singapore’s planted architecture as much as its skyline.

  • The planted atrium gives the city hotel its garden-in-a-hotel character.
  • Marina Bay is the setting, not a beach-resort location.
  • Peach Blossoms and Peppermint offer different Chinese and international dining possibilities.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
City travellers interested in planted architecture and Marina Bay, with dining close to the room.

A stay, imagined

Begin with a Marina Bay walk before the heat encourages a slower pace. Return to the planted atrium for lunch, letting the hotel’s green interior become part of the city experience rather than simply somewhere to sleep. Another afternoon could turn towards museums or neighbourhood dining with a guide. Reserve Peach Blossoms for a Chinese dinner, or choose a less formal meal according to the group. The hotel suits a city stay where greenery and architecture matter alongside the practical location.
