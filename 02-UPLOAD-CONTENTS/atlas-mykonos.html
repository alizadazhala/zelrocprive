How the stay could unfold

A sheltered villa can make the island feel intimate even in summer. We can coordinate a chef, provisions, housekeeping and transport, then explore sailing days and restaurant reservations according to your preferred balance of sociable evenings and quiet mornings.

Share the size of your group, approximate timing and any small details that would make the stay yours. We explore possibilities across budgets; you choose what suits you, from one reservation to the whole journey.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.

Tell us which setting or property appeals, approximate dates and how many people are travelling. You do not need a complete itinerary.
