102 Dongling Road, Yangshuo, Guilin, Guangxi, China

Aurua Yangshuo Sugar House

A former sugar factory on the Li River gives industrial architecture a setting among Yangshuo’s karst peaks.

  • Preserved factory structures include trusses, workshops and the former pressing room.
  • Rooms, villas and historic suites offer different relationships to the converted buildings.
  • A riverside pool, Sugar House Restaurant and 1969 Bar make the factory complex a destination itself.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Design enthusiasts who want a substantial riverside hotel alongside countryside exploration.

A stay, imagined

Walk through the old factory spaces before setting out into Yangshuo’s landscape. A guided countryside outing can occupy the morning without turning every day into a transfer; leave enough time to return while the light still catches the karst peaks. Back at the hotel, the riverside pool and the industrial structures give the afternoon a different texture. Dinner can stay within the complex, with a later drink at 1969 Bar if it is operating for your dates. Choose the room’s setting as carefully as the category.
