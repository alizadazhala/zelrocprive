Canyon Point

Canyon Point, Kane County, southern Utah, United States

Amangiri

Low concrete suites turn the sandstone desert into a continuous presence, indoors and out.

  • Each main-resort suite has an outdoor lounge and fireplace; pools belong to selected categories.
  • The published suite programme includes full board for two, scheduled group hikes and wellness classes.
  • Camp Sarika is a separate tented-pavilion choice, not the accommodation in the main resort.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Architecture-minded couples and travellers who want desert walks with a substantial retreat to return to.

A stay, imagined

Walk while the desert is still cool, then return to the suite as the light changes across the stone. The afternoon need not become another expedition: the outdoor lounge, a swim or a reserved treatment can give it a different pace. A slot-canyon visit belongs on a separate, properly guided outing, with any permits established beforehand. As evening settles, the fireplace and the shape of the mesas do most of the work. Choose your room for its view and layout; a private pool is a specific category.

Big Water & Lake Powell country

Big Water area, Kane County, Utah, United States

Under Canvas Lake Powell–Grand Staircase

Canvas tents on a canyon-rim plateau put outdoor living ahead of conventional hotel facilities.

  • Safari-inspired tents stand on raised decks and have private bathrooms.
  • The setting includes an on-site slot canyon and views towards Grand Staircase.
  • A shared lounge and camp dining make the experience more communal than an isolated suite.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Travellers who enjoy camping-style accommodation, desert outings and time outside.

A stay, imagined

The day begins with canvas, cool air and a horizon that asks you to get outdoors. Set off for a planned canyon or lake outing, taking account of heat and the access rules at each stop. Back at camp, the shared spaces give the evening a sociable shape, while your tent keeps a quieter retreat close by. This is a different proposition from a pool suite at Amangiri: pack for the season, enjoy the outdoor rhythm and let the desert remain part of the night.

How the stay could unfold

Give the first morning to the sandstone close to your base. A guided walk makes the scale more legible; a longer park day needs a separate route rather than an assumption that everything is nearby.

The choice between a suite and a tent is about how you want to inhabit the landscape. Allow heat, daylight and road distances to shape the day, with the pool or the camp deck waiting on your return.
