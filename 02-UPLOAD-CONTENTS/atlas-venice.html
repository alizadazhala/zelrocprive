Giudecca, Venice, Veneto, Italy

Airelles Palladio, Venice

A garden-and-lagoon retreat across the water from the central visitor routes.

  • The sixteenth-century property occupies Giudecca rather than the San Marco side.
  • Rooms and suites include specifically named St Mark’s-view categories and a separate three-bedroom pool villa.
  • The Guerlain spa and Matsuhisa restaurant make time at the property part of the stay.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Guests who value gardens and spa time, and are comfortable using boats for central Venice outings.

A stay, imagined

Cross the lagoon with the final boat transfer already arranged, then leave the first afternoon for Giudecca and the gardens. A central Venice morning can follow, with the return timed before everyone becomes tired of walking. Back at Palladio, a spa appointment or dinner at Matsuhisa gives the property an evening of its own. A view of St Mark’s belongs to particular accommodation categories, so choose that deliberately. The attraction is Venice from across the water, with a little separation when the day ends.
