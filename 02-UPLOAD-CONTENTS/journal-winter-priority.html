Journal / Winter notebook

The one thing you would love to do

A small note to keep before the next winter escape.

Sometimes one detail gives a winter trip its shape. A dog-sledding afternoon, a first ski lesson, or a mountain lunch with the whole family can matter more than a long itinerary.

Share that wish when you first enquire. Experiences such as dog sledding in Val d’Isère can fill before arrival, and snow, age requirements and suitability also shape the options.

We explore the experience you care about early, with alternatives if it is unavailable. Leave the rest of the week open enough for fresh snow, a slower morning or another hour at the spa.
