Médran & Place Blanche

Place Blanche, Médran, Verbier, Valais, Switzerland

W Verbier

A lively Médran base for guests who want skiing immediately outside.

  • Ski-in, ski-out access places the hotel beside the main lift hub.
  • Rooms feature fireplaces and balconies; residences offer kitchens and extra bedrooms.
  • Le Spa W and Brasserie 1519 give the time beyond skiing its own focus.

A stay, imagined

Leave breakfast for the lift without first organising a drive across the village. An instructor can meet the group around its different abilities, while confident skiers plan a longer mountain day with a guide. Return to Place Blanche for a slower afternoon at the spa, or keep the social rhythm going over a drink. The residences suggest another way to stay: enough space for friends to gather, with breakfast at home before everyone chooses their own route.

The village & its quieter edges

Route de Verbier Station, Verbier village, Valais, Switzerland

Experimental Chalet Verbier

A compact village hotel for guests who put a sociable evening alongside a ski day.

  • Le St Bernard is the current restaurant name, with a French-Swiss Alpine approach.
  • The hotel is in the village rather than directly on a piste.
  • The published shuttle connects guests with lifts around 600 metres away.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Guests whose priorities match this setting and the style described below.

A stay, imagined

Let the morning begin in the village, with the shuttle and equipment already considered. The mountain can take most of the day, or only the first few hours if lunch and time in Verbier appeal more. Later, return to the chalet rather than a large resort lobby. Dinner at Le St Bernard keeps the evening close to home, with the option to continue into the village. This is a base for enjoying Verbier’s social life as well as its altitude.

24 Rue du Centre Sportif, Verbier, Valais, Switzerland

La Cordée des Alpes

A smaller village hotel with apartments for groups who want extra room to settle in.

  • The hotel lists 32 rooms and suites alongside six larger apartments.
  • Its spa has a 15-metre indoor pool.
  • The restaurant offers a Valais brasserie setting rather than a mountain lunch stop.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Guests whose priorities match this setting and the style described below.

A stay, imagined

Choose an apartment if the group wants a shared sitting room and room for different morning routines. Some might head for a lesson while others walk through Verbier before meeting for lunch. The indoor pool gives a mixed group a comfortable alternative to an extra afternoon on skis. Come together again at the hotel restaurant, or reserve a table elsewhere in the village. The attraction is a manageable base that can accommodate several ways of spending the same day.

Le Chalet d’Adrien

An intimate chalet hotel for guests who want the mountain view and dinner to carry as much weight as skiing.

  • The hotel has 29 rooms.
  • The spa includes a pool, hammam and Clarins treatments.
  • La Table d’Adrien gives a stay a distinct restaurant focus.

Who it suits
Guests whose priorities match this setting and the style described below.

A stay, imagined

Reserve one evening for La Table d’Adrien before filling the rest of the stay. The next morning can then be free for a guide-led ski day, a lesson or a gentler outing from Verbier. Back at the chalet, take time for the pool and a treatment rather than rushing straight out again. A room chosen for its outlook makes an afternoon indoors appealing too. The scale suits guests who prefer returning to a small hotel after a substantial mountain day.
