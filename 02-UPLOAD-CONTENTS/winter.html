The details that make the difference

Share the things
you would love to do.

A first ski lesson, dog sledding, a particular mountain lunch, or simply a chalet where everyone can be together. Tell us early, so we can explore availability before arrival.

Hotels near the slopes, private chalets and smaller lodges give different ways to spend your budget. We coordinate equipment, instruction, dining and non-skiing days around couples, families and groups of friends.

Flying experiences are considered individually. Gstaad has locally listed tandem paragliding, winter ballooning and scenic helicopter possibilities; each needs operator, weather and suitability checks.
