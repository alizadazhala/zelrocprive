How the stay could unfold

Wake near the slopes, with equipment fitting and instruction arranged around your group. Experienced skiers can explore with an appropriate guide while beginners find their confidence; everyone can meet for an unhurried lunch. The afternoon might belong to the mountain, the spa or a book by the fire.

A private chalet brings friends and families together over breakfast without a timetable and dinner prepared around their tastes. A hotel or residence can offer a different balance of space and service. We compare atmosphere, location, direct slope access where available and budget before you choose.

For winter 2026/27, Bourbon Hotel in Courchevel 1850 announces a December opening, direct ski access, a spa and the Italian restaurant Simonetta. Hôtel Saint Roch, also in 1850, is announced for 18 December 2026, with a Tata Harper spa and pool. These are forthcoming openings, with operating details to reconfirm for your stay.

Sundance and Steamboat are hotel residences in Courchevel Moriond, announced for December 2026: La Bokka is the Italian dining option at Sundance; Arvi is the Savoyard restaurant announced at Steamboat. Bârma is a hotel, residence and spa in La Tania. ZIA at Sivolière is a restaurant within the 1850 hotel; we will check its seasonal service dates when planning your evening.

Bagatelle Courchevel is a mountain restaurant at Sommet de la Loze, rather than accommodation. Its official page currently says “Reopening Soon”; we will explore seasonal dates and lunch reservations before including it in your plan. Tell us early about any experience that matters most, so we can explore availability before you arrive.
