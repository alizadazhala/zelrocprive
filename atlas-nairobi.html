Lang’ata

Lang’ata, Nairobi, Kenya

Giraffe Manor

A small manor within a giraffe sanctuary makes conservation and the house’s resident setting part of the visit.

  • Historic Manor and Garden Manor are distinct wings with individually named rooms.
  • Dining is prepared for expected resident guests rather than operated as a public à-la-carte restaurant.
  • The nearby Retreat provides a pool and a different space to pause during a manor stay.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Wildlife-focused travellers and families considering a short Nairobi stay before or after safari.

A stay, imagined

Spend the morning with the house and its sanctuary setting, allowing the animals’ own behaviour to determine what happens. Breakfast is a resident occasion, not a restaurant booking that can be substituted for an overnight stay. Later, choose a neighbourhood visit or time beside the Retreat’s pool before returning for dinner. If safari follows, build the onward transfer around the actual flight rather than an optimistic city crossing. The pleasure is in staying long enough to notice the place, without treating an encounter as an appointment.

Karen

Mbagathi Ridge, Karen, Nairobi, Kenya

Hemingways Nairobi

Generous suites and gardens make leafy Karen a restful Nairobi base beyond the city centre.

  • The hotel has 45 suites rather than a high-rise room inventory.
  • Private balconies and Ngong Hills outlooks give the accommodation a residential feel.
  • Karen’s location suits cultural visits and a considered pause around a safari itinerary.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Couples or families who prefer spacious suites and a quieter suburban base.

A stay, imagined

Let an arrival day stay gentle: settle into the suite, step onto the balcony and decide whether lunch in Karen is enough of an outing. A cultural visit can occupy the next morning, with a driver already considering the return and any later flight. Leave the garden and a proper dinner at the hotel as possibilities rather than obligations. Unlike a stay chosen around a particular wildlife interaction, this is a Nairobi pause that can adapt to jet lag, family routines and the next stage of the journey.
