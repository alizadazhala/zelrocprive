The Beekman, A Thompson Hotel

A downtown hotel whose historic atrium gives the return from the city a distinct atmosphere.

  • The landmark Temple Court building contains a nine-storey historic atrium.
  • Lower Manhattan provides a different base from a Midtown hotel.
  • Temple Court, The Bar Room and Le Gratin create contrasting dining choices.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Culture and architecture travellers whose plans favour Lower Manhattan.

A stay, imagined

Give the morning to Lower Manhattan rather than starting every day with a trip uptown. A walk, a museum or a long lunch can lead back to the atrium for a quieter pause. Later, choose between dinner at Temple Court and a different mood at Le Gratin, with the reservation matched to the evening. A performance elsewhere in the city can follow on another day. The building is the reason to stay here, while the downtown address should fit the places you actually want to visit.
