Four Seasons Hotel Gresham Palace Budapest

Restored Art Nouveau interiors meet a Danube address at the Pest end of the Chain Bridge.

  • The 1906 building retains glass, ironwork, stained glass and mosaic detail.
  • Its position faces the river and the Chain Bridge, rather than a secluded resort setting.
  • Rooms with river outlooks and other categories create a meaningful view-versus-budget choice.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Architecture enthusiasts and couples planning a walkable city break with a river-facing base.

A stay, imagined

Step out towards the Chain Bridge after breakfast and let the first day follow the river rather than a sequence of car journeys. A museum or guided architectural walk can give the afternoon its focus, with a return to the hotel’s Art Nouveau interiors before dinner. Choose a river-view room if watching the city change after dark matters to you; another category can leave more room for dining and cultural plans. A bathhouse visit elsewhere in Budapest is a separate experience, not the same as the hotel spa.
