Beşiktaş

Beşiktaş, Istanbul

Four Seasons Hotel Istanbul at the Bosphorus

Explore this stay +

A palace setting on the European waterfront, with room for pool time between city outings.

Accommodation
Rooms and suites include different outlooks. Specify a Bosphorus-view category if seeing the water from your room is central to the stay.
Facilities
A historic palace building, waterfront outdoor pool, indoor pool and spa with hammam treatments.
Dining
Aqua is the hotel’s Mediterranean restaurant with close views of the Bosphorus: a natural option for an evening near your room.
Operation
City stays can be explored across seasons; outdoor pool and terrace operation must be checked for the chosen dates.

Choose this for palace atmosphere and a Beşiktaş base. It is a different proposition from a hotel in the old city: plan transport for major sights rather than assuming everything is a short walk.

Kuruçeşme

Kuruçeşme, between Ortaköy and Bebek, Istanbul

Mandarin Oriental Bosphorus, Istanbul

Explore this stay +

A contemporary waterfront base for guests who want time at the hotel alongside the city.

Accommodation
Rooms, connecting and family choices, and suites. Confirm the precise view category and layout rather than assuming every room faces the Bosphorus.

Karaköy

Karaköy, Istanbul

The Peninsula Istanbul

Explore this stay +

A waterfront city base for guests who want to combine hotel time with the streets around Karaköy.

Accommodation
Rooms and suites in a waterfront hotel complex. We compare specific layouts and outlooks for couples, families or friends.
Facilities
A Bosphorus-front outdoor pool and indoor pool with hammam and spa facilities.
Dining
GALLADA by Fatih Tutak offers Turk-Asian cooking on the rooftop; Topside Bar provides another way to make an evening of the view.
Operation
Restaurant service days, terrace use and outdoor pool operation are checked for the season; no reopening or renovation claims are assumed from older announcements.

Choose Karaköy if its city setting appeals more than staying farther along the shore. Allow a separate day for historic sights and leave time for local walks instead of spending every day in a car.

How the stay could unfold

Arrive with time for a waterfront pause rather than another appointment. If you stay in Beşiktaş, Aqua can make the first evening feel close to home. At Kuruçeşme, compare a Novikov dinner with the simpler pleasure of remaining at the hotel after the journey.

Give the next day to the historic city with a guide and transfers planned around your interests. A few places explored properly leave room for lunch and the ride back. On another morning, take to the Bosphorus with a private boat if conditions allow, watching the shoreline change from the water.

For an evening in Karaköy, explore a reservation at GALLADA or a drink at Topside Bar, then return by the route agreed in advance. Dining requests, boat bookings and room views are all confirmed individually: the shape of the stay is yours to choose.

What we can coordinate

  • Airport transfers and drivers between neighbourhoods, museums and evening plans
  • Bosphorus-view room requests and suitable family or connecting layouts
  • Guided cultural visits and a realistic pace between sights
  • Restaurant and nightlife reservations, dietary needs and celebration details
  • Private boat enquiries, with conditions and route confirmed
  • Group arrangements, or a city stay followed by the Mediterranean or Aegean coast

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
