How the stay could unfold

In Oxford, step out onto the High before the day grows busy. A guided walk leaves time for a bookshop, lunch and a museum; the afternoon can remain open.

At Estelle Manor, the day can revolve around the estate: breakfast together, a walk, and a bathhouse session requested in advance. A cabin or house gives a family or group a different rhythm from a room in the manor.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, London or airport transfers and a driver for outings
  • Dining and cultural visits around opening calendars
  • Spa requests, estate activities and family arrangements
  • Cabins or houses for groups, with services confirmed for the chosen stay

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
