The Atlas / Mediterranean chapters

01 / France

French Riviera

The coast, between the moments.

Pine-scented paths. Shaded terraces. An afternoon that stretches beyond lunch.

A journey shaped for you

Pale light on the water, pine-scented paths and an afternoon that stretches beyond lunch. The Riviera rewards a slower rhythm: a base near the sea, time in the hill villages, and evenings with nowhere else to be.

We can shape a stay around a villa or hotel, a coastal walk, a boat-day enquiry and a considered dining plan. Tell us whether you prefer the energy of Saint-Tropez or quieter corners of the coast; we will account for transfers and the season.

02 / Italy

Amalfi Coast

Life above the water.

Terraced gardens, stone steps and room between the towns.

A journey shaped for you

Terraced gardens, stone steps and the sea at the end of every view. Along the Amalfi Coast, the pleasure is in leaving space between the towns; on Capri, early mornings and late afternoons reveal a gentler island.

A bespoke brief might pair a coastal residence with garden visits, a local guide and time on the water. We consider steps, luggage, road journeys and ferry schedules alongside the view, and explore private transfers where appropriate.

04 / Greece

Mykonos

Follow the light.

Sociable or secluded. Your own rhythm, from morning light to evening.

A journey shaped for you

Whitewashed lanes soften in the morning light; later, the island opens out into wind, sea and long horizons. Mykonos can be sociable or secluded, depending on where you stay and how you choose to spend the day.

We can research a sheltered villa setting, arrange transport, enquire about a sailing day and plan dinners around your preferred pace. Family time, a celebration or quiet mornings form different briefs; weather and seasonal availability guide the details.
