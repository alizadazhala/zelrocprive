Who it suits

A stay, imagined

Begin on the snowfront with the group’s instruction and equipment already considered. Confident skiers can leave for a longer route while beginners find their own rhythm, meeting later for an Alpine lunch. An afternoon at the house can be as appealing as another run, especially when younger guests have their own spaces to enjoy. Dinner might turn Italian at Palladio or Japanese-Peruvian at Matsuhisa on an operating evening. Choose an apartment when shared living space matters, keeping everyone’s time together easy without requiring identical days.

How the stay could unfold

Begin close to the lifts, in a hotel chosen for its location or a private chalet with room for everyone. Equipment can be fitted and lessons timed to the group, with separate plans for confident skiers and those trying their first turns. Meet over mountain lunch, then choose another run or an afternoon at the spa.

Non-skiers can explore winter walks or a gentle village day. Dog sledding in the Manchet Valley is another possibility listed by the local tourism office. It is the kind of experience worth mentioning early: spaces can fill before arrival, and we will explore dates, conditions and suitability with the operator.

For couples, dinner might be a quiet table for two; for friends, an evening out with the return arranged; for a family, a chef-led chalet supper after a full day outside. Your preferred pace shapes the plan.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
