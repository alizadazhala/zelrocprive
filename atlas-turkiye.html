Türkiye / Mediterranean coast / Antalya

Belek, Antalya

Beachfront resorts and established golf courses make Belek a useful choice for a mixed party: some guests play, others swim, spend time at the spa or settle into a family holiday.

Golf trips, multi-generational resort holidays and couples who want different plans within the same stay.

Türkiye / Mediterranean coast / Kiriş, Kemer

Kemer, Antalya

Choose Kemer for a stay centred on a bay, beach time and the changing colour of the Mediterranean. It suits guests who would rather settle into one coastal base than organise a different outing every day.

Couples seeking beach-and-spa days, families, and groups comparing suites with a more private villa.

Türkiye / Aegean coast / Bodrum peninsula

Bodrum, Muğla

Bodrum gives a coastal stay a more outward-looking rhythm: a resort base, a day on the water and an evening elsewhere on the peninsula. Maxx Royal is in Göltürkbükü, rather than in Bodrum town itself.

Dining-led summer stays, boat days, couples and friends, or families wanting a spacious villa base.

How the stay could unfold

A morning round. A different afternoon for everyone.

One guest leaves for a tee time at Montgomerie or Carya; another takes breakfast slowly before swimming. Children have their own morning, with age-appropriate activities checked in advance. Meet for lunch rather than asking the whole party to follow the same schedule.

On a non-golf day, leave the clubs aside. Choose the beach, a treatment or time together by the pool. For a dedicated golf trip, reverse the emphasis: plan course bookings and tuition first, then fit meals, recovery time and a quieter evening around them.

Three beaches, and no need to hurry between them.

Begin with breakfast before choosing where to swim. The appeal here is repetition with small variations: a different place by the water, a longer lunch, a spa appointment while someone else stays outside. A lagoon suite and a waterside villa make quite different bases for the same day.

Later, choose dinner from the restaurants operating that evening, or keep the meal flexible at Twenty4. If a celebration is the reason for coming, we can discuss a suitable table, flowers and the arrangements around the group rather than turning every day into an occasion.

An Aegean day with room for an evening out.

Leave the first morning for the beach. On another day, explore a skippered boat option: a short outing with lunch or a longer route, chosen around your group, budget and the conditions. There is no need to charter a larger yacht if a smaller vessel suits the day.

Return with time to change before Casa Sol or a reservation enquiry at Spago. Another evening could take you beyond the hotel with a driver arranged for the return. For a villa stay, ask about permitted dining and chef services before imagining a completely independent household.

Begin with the Bosphorus. Give the city a day of its own.

A waterfront breakfast can be followed by a guided cultural day, with enough time for the journeys between neighbourhoods. Leave the next morning for a boat outing or a walk closer to the hotel. A city break feels different when every hour is not a timed appointment.

  • Airport transfers and drivers, with flight details and journey times checked

  • Restaurant requests, dietary requirements and celebration arrangements
  • Boat outings and other activities matched to season, conditions and budget
  • Villa provisions and private-chef enquiries where the selected property permits them
