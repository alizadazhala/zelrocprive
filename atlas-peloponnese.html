Near Porto Heli, Argolis, Peloponnese, Greece

Amanzoe

A hilltop pavilion with its own pool makes the olive-covered Peloponnese the centre of the stay.

  • Pool pavilions and larger villas occupy the hill; beach pavilions offer a different setting.
  • The beach club is a separate coastal outing, rather than sand immediately outside every room.
  • Classical colonnades frame olive groves and the Aegean; cultural visits can complement sea days.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Couples seeking privacy; families comparing a villa with separate pavilions.

A stay, imagined

Start with the olive groves below your terrace, before the day becomes warm enough for a swim in your own pool. Later, change the setting entirely: leave the hill for the beach club and a lunch close to the water. Another day could turn inland towards the Peloponnese’s archaeological landscapes, with the route chosen before you set off. Return for a quiet evening at the pavilion, or reserve a hotel dinner. There is space here for both an outing and a day without one.
