Families / Friends / Celebrations

The pleasure
of being together.

Explore winter chalets ↗

A large family, friends marking an occasion, or a few days with no reason beyond time together. We seek properties according to group size, bedroom arrangements, setting and budget, from a comfortable private house to a more expansive staffed villa. In winter, private chalets offer the same intimacy close to the mountains.

A personal chef can shape menus around your preferences, dietary needs and daily rhythm. Groceries before arrival, housekeeping, airport transfers and restaurant reservations can all be explored alongside the property, with services and staffing confirmed for the home you choose.

We can coordinate activities, boat days, a birthday dinner, flowers or a thoughtful gift. Arrange one detail or ask us to bring the entire stay together. Your group chooses the balance of independence and attention.

Begin with an approximate group size, preferred dates and any early ideas. You do not need to have chosen a destination.
