Fogo Island Inn

Ocean-facing rooms and locally made interiors connect a contemporary inn to an outport community.

  • All 29 rooms and suites face the North Atlantic.
  • Furniture and textiles are made by island artisans and makers.
  • Published full-board stays include meals and most land-based cultural activities; boat outings need separate checks.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Design enthusiasts and travellers interested in island life, food and a slower northern coast.

A stay, imagined

Wake to the North Atlantic, then let the island supply the morning: a local walk, a conversation about fishing or a closer look at the furniture made here. Lunch can be followed by a drive between settlements, with weather deciding how long you stay outside. Return before dinner to watch the horizon from your room. A boat outing is another possibility when conditions and the season support it, not a reason to rush the crossing. Give this island several nights rather than treating it as a stopover.

How the stay could unfold

A morning walk follows the shoreline before lunch brings the island’s produce back to the table. A community-hosted encounter or guided outing can deepen the afternoon without filling it.

Allow for the ferry and the weather in both directions. This is a seasonal island journey, with the surrounding settlements as important as the building you came to see.
