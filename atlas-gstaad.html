Begin above the village, then let the day divide between skiing and a slower morning at the spa. Meeting for lunch need not mean that everyone follows the same mountain route. A driver can connect a further outing, but the hillside hotel is also a reason to spend part of the stay happily at home.

How the stay could unfold

A chalet breakfast can stretch while the group decides on the day. Skiers leave for lessons or a guided outing; others choose a winter walk, village shops or the spa. We can explore hotels with convenient skiing access and private chalets, checking the actual route to the slopes for your group.

An unhurried mountain lunch is a meeting point rather than a deadline. Later, couples can retreat to a quiet table while a family might settle in for a chef-prepared supper. Instruction, equipment and transport can accommodate different ages and levels of confidence.

For another perspective, Gstaad’s tourism office lists tandem paragliding, winter ballooning and winter helicopter sightseeing. We explore the specific operator, season, weather and guest suitability before proposing a flight. Nearby Château-d’Œx also offers a January balloon-festival journey, with attendance and any passenger flight considered separately.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation, airport transfers and drivers
  • Dining reservations and evenings planned around your preferences
  • Local activities, cultural visits and seasonal outings
  • Private stays, chef requests and celebrations where supported by the chosen property
  • Arrangements for couples, families and groups, with costs and terms discussed personally

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
