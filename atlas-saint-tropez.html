Rue Gambetta, Saint-Tropez village, Var, France

Pan Deï Palais

A small garden house in the village for guests who want Saint-Tropez on foot.

  • Twelve rooms give the house an intimate scale.
  • A garden and mosaic pool sit behind the village address.
  • Les Délices du Pan Deï offers dining at home; Jardin Tropézina is a separate beach outing.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Couples who want a small village house and Saint-Tropez on foot.

A stay, imagined

Step into Saint-Tropez before the streets become busy, returning to the garden for a quieter lunch or afternoon by the pool. A beach day can follow with transport and the reservation already arranged at Jardin Tropézina. Back in the village, the evening can remain spontaneous in mood even when the table has been considered in advance. Choose Pan Deï for the pleasure of returning on foot to a small house. A hilltop estate or private villa would create a different relationship with the town.

The hills above Saint-Tropez, Var, France

Château de la Messardière

A Mediterranean estate above the town for guests who want the property to be a substantial part of the holiday.

  • The hilltop park overlooks the Pampelonne area rather than sitting directly on its beach.
  • The Valmont spa provides an alternative to an afternoon outside.
  • Le Palladio gives an Italian-Mediterranean evening at the hotel; Jardin Tropézina is a separate coastal setting.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Guests planning substantial time at an estate, with village and beach outings as separate choices.

A stay, imagined

Let breakfast and the park occupy the morning before deciding whether to leave for the beach. A prearranged Jardin Tropézina reservation can make lunch by the sea easy to enjoy, with the return already considered. Another day can remain at the château, taking in the spa and a longer pause before dinner at Le Palladio. Choose this estate for time within it, not simply as somewhere to sleep after a village evening. Services and any dining inclusions should follow the exact room programme booked.
