Gordes, Luberon, Provence, France

La Bastide de Gordes

A village-edge house whose terraces give the Luberon valley a place in every unhurried day.

  • The sixteenth-century building has terraced gardens above the valley.
  • Village-facing and valley-facing rooms offer different outlooks.
  • La Table de la Bastide, Clover Gordes, La Bastide de Pierres and Beefbar give different dining settings.
Explore this stay +

Who it suits
Couples and families who prefer a Provençal village and valley views to a beachfront holiday.

A stay, imagined

Walk through Gordes before the day grows warm, then leave the afternoon for the terraces and the valley view. A village lunch might become the day’s only appointment, with a separate outing to another part of the Luberon tomorrow. Dinner can stay close, chosen from the house’s different restaurant settings. The right room matters: a village outlook and a valley outlook will frame the morning differently. Choose this base for Provence and its village rhythm, rather than as another stop on a coastal itinerary.
