How the stay could unfold

Set out for Giza with a guide and a plan suited to the season, leaving the rest of the day generous. A Nile-side dinner can follow on a different evening, with travel time considered before the table is booked.

In Luxor, give one set of sites a morning rather than trying to see everything. Return to a shaded courtyard and a late lunch. A separate Red Sea stay can follow, with diving explored for the guest’s ability and the conditions.

What we can coordinate

  • Accommodation and airport transfers, including onward connections
  • Drivers, local guides and cultural visits around opening calendars
  • Dining, wellness and experiences selected around your preferences
  • Villas, family arrangements and celebrations where supported by the property
  • Changes and personal requests throughout the stay

Our attention continues beyond the reservation. Contact us 24/7 during your stay, including for a change of plan or a spontaneous idea. We explore suitable options and confirm the arrangements you choose.
